**Diplomatic Transcription — Folio 0271** (``0271.jpg``, Sheet 1 of 11):

Derives :math:`x = \frac{d}{2} + \frac{r_1^2-r_2^2}{2d}` and tests :math:`r_1=1, r_2=2, d=5 \implies x=2.2, x'=2.8, t = \frac{4\sqrt{6}}{5}`.
