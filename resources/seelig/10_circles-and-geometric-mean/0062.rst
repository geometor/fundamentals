**Diplomatic Transcription — Folio 0062** (``0062.jpg``, Sheet 1 of 4):

Expresses :math:`P^2 = (3+Q)(3-Q)` and :math:`P^2 = (5+(Z-Q))(5-(Z-Q))` as semicircle geometric means and derives :math:`Z(Q-P) = 12` and :math:`Q = \frac{Z^2-16}{2Z}`.
