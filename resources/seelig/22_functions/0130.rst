   \Phi(x) = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2}\operatorname{erf}\left(\frac{x}{\sqrt{2}}\right), \qquad \operatorname{erf}(x) \approx 1 - \left(\sum_{k=1}^5 a_k t^k\right) e^{-x^2}, \quad t = \frac{1}{1 + 0.3275911 x}
