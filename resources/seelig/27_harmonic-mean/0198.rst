**Diplomatic Transcription — Folio 0198** (``0198.jpg``, Sheet 16 of 19):

Solves :math:`Y^2 + (X+Z)Y - XZ = 0` for the internal segment :math:`Y = CB`.

.. math::

   Y^2 + (X + Z) Y - X Z = 0 \implies Y = \frac{-(X + Z) + \sqrt{(X + Z)^2 + 4 X Z}}{2}
