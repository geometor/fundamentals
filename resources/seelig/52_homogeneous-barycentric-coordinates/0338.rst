**Diplomatic Transcription — Folio 0338** (``0338.jpg``, Sheet 8 of 15):

Expands the symmetric polynomials :math:`P = abc, U = a^2b^2+b^2c^2+c^2a^2, S_4 = a^4+b^4+c^4` and proves :math:`\lambda_A + \lambda_B + \lambda_C = 1`.
