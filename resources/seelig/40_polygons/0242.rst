Expresses :math:`E_{10} = \phi^{-1} = \sqrt{2-\phi}`, :math:`E_5 = \sqrt{3-\phi}`, and :math:`E_{20} = \sqrt{2-\sqrt{2+\phi}}` in terms of :math:`\phi = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}`.
