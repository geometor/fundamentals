Changelog¶

0.1.0 (2026-11-02)¶

  • Archival Migration (`resources/`): - Migrated all 55 handwritten geometry notebook topics (353 scanned folios) by Fred W. Seelig from the legacy Grav pages/ hierarchy into resources/seelig/<NN_slug>/ with sequential 4-digit folio identifiers (0001.jpg–0353.jpg), diplomatic .rst transcriptions, .notes.rst commentary, and folio_reviews.json. - Migrated OpenDocument spreadsheets (Triangles and Circles.ods with 59 worksheets, circumcircle-circumcenter.ods, triangle-basics-draw.ods) into resources/spreadsheets/ and extracted worksheets.json.

  • 3-Track Book Codex (`docsrc/topics/f-01`–`f-55`): - Compiled Track 1 — Synthesis (`index.rst`) with formal .. enunciation::, interactive Explorer-styled SVG diagrams (f-NN.svg), .. signature:: blocks, SymPy derivations on the canonical (a, b, c) = (5, 6, 7) triangle, collapsible spreadsheet tables, Graphviz dependency DAGs, and cross-links to geometor/euclid, geometor/elements (G.<n>), and geometor/etc (X(n)). - Compiled Track 2 — Folios (`folios.rst`) pairing all 353 manuscript scans with .. folio:: markers, diplomatic mathematical transcriptions, and editorial notes. - Compiled Track 3 — Code (`code.rst`) with standalone executable model.py scripts using geometor.model and sympy.

  • Scholarly Apparatus: - Added resources/glossary.json, resources/persons.json, and resources/citations.json and generated docsrc/glossary.rst, docsrc/persons.rst, and docsrc/bibliography.rst.

0.0.1 (2023-11-28)¶

  • Initial repository scaffold and raw archival import.
