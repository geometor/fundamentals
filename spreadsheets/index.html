Spreadsheets¶

Alongside his 353 handwritten notebook pages, seelig constructed an extensive suite of computational geometry workbooks in OpenDocument Spreadsheet (.ods) format. These sheets test algebraic identities, verify homogeneous barycentric coordinates, and compute exact and numerical coordinates for the canonical \((a, b, c) = (5, 6, 7)\) reference triangle.

Workbooks in Archive¶

  1. resources/spreadsheets/Triangles and Circles.ods — Master workbook containing 59 worksheets spanning triangle centers, circle families, harmonic conjugates, projective theorems, and special triangles.

  2. resources/spreadsheets/00.triangle-basics/circumcircle-circumcenter.ods — Step-by-step derivation of the circumcenter and circumcircle from side lengths \((5, 6, 7)\) (also exported as .csv, .html, .fods, and .xlsx).

  3. resources/spreadsheets/00.triangle-basics/triangle-basics-draw.ods — Coordinate plotting workbook for basic triangle vertices and centers.
