F.40 Regular Polygons and Nested Radicals¶

Archimedean Edge-Doubling Recursion, Golden Decagon/Pentagon Radicals, and the Pentadecagon

For a regular N-gon inscribed in a unit circle (R = 1), doubling the number of sides maps edge length E_{N/2} to E_N = sqrt(2 - sqrt(4 - E_{N/2}^2)), generating closed-form nested quadratic radicals for the 2^k, 3*2^k, 5*2^k, and 15*2^k polygon families.

O : Point A : Point ===== c_unit = circle(O, A) l_oa = line(O, A) perp = perpendicular(O, l_oa) B = intersection(perp, c_unit) M = midpoint(O, B) c_five = circle(M, A)

Mathematical Synthesis¶

Spanning 18 folios (SeeligNotebooks), Seelig’s treatise on regular polygons derives the Archimedean edge-doubling recursion \(E_N = \sqrt{2 - \sqrt{4 - E_{N/2}^2}}\) (for circumradius \(R=1\), where \(E_N = 2\sin(\pi/N)\) and apothem \(r_N = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4 - E_N^2}\)) and compiles complete nested-radical tables across four constructible families: the square/octagon chain (\(E_4 = \sqrt{2}, E_8 = \sqrt{2-\sqrt{2}}, E_{16}, E_{32}, E_{64}\)), the triangle/hexagon/dodecagon chain (\(E_3 = \sqrt{3}, E_6 = 1, E_{12} = \sqrt{2-\sqrt{3}}, E_{24}\)), and the golden ratio pentagon/decagon chain (\(E_5 = \sqrt{3-\phi} = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{10-2\sqrt{5}} \approx 1.17557, E_{10} = \sqrt{2-\phi} = \phi - 1 \approx 0.61803, E_{20} = \sqrt{2-\sqrt{2+\phi}}\)).

Dated April 20, 2015, the second half of the dossier investigates the regular 15-gon (Euclid Elements IV.16, \(E_{15} = 2\sin 12^\circ \approx 0.41582\)), citing James Stafford’s exact radical table for multiples of \(\pi/30 = 6^\circ\), alongside an angle-chasing exploration of the regular enneagon (\(N=9\), requiring a cubic trisection) and two auxiliary Cartesian cevian sheets.

Key Algebraic Identities¶

\[E_N = 2\sin\frac{\pi}{N} = \sqrt{2 - \sqrt{4 - E_{N/2}^2}}, \qquad r_N = \cos\frac{\pi}{N} = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4 - E_N^2}\]
\[E_{10} = \phi - 1 = \sqrt{2 - \phi}, \qquad E_5 = \sqrt{3 - \phi}, \qquad E_{15} = 2\sin 12^\circ = \frac{1}{4}\left(\sqrt{10 + 2\sqrt{5}} + \sqrt{3} - \sqrt{15}\right)\]
