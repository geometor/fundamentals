F.23 Golden Triangles — Folios¶

Archival manuscript scans and diplomatic transcriptions from seelig’s notebooks for F.23 (Golden Triangles) (SeeligNotebooks).

Folio 0132 (Page 1 of 3): Golden Triangle 72-72-36 & Segment AD¶

[Folio 0132]

Diplomatic Mathematical & Geometric Transcription

Diplomatic Transcription — Folio 0132 (0132.jpg, Sheet 1 of 3):

Analyzes the \(72^\circ\text{–}72^\circ\text{–}36^\circ\) triangle with legs \(\phi\), base \(1\), and point \(D\) dividing \(BC\) into \(\phi-1\) and \(1\), proving \(AD = 2\sin 36^\circ \approx 1.1756\).

\[\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}, \qquad \cos 36^\circ = \frac{\phi}{2}, \qquad \cos 72^\circ = \frac{\phi - 1}{2} = \frac{1}{2\phi}, \qquad 2\sin 36^\circ = \sqrt{3 - \phi}\]
