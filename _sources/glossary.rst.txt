.. _glossary:

========
Glossary
========

Canonical geometric definitions and concepts referenced across the 55
topics of the Fundamentals codex.

.. glossary::
   :sorted:

   Apollonian circles
   Apollonian circle
   circle of Apollonius
   circles of Apollonius
      A family of circles defined as the locus of points whose distances from two fixed foci A and B have a constant ratio k, or the three circles of a triangle passing through a vertex and two isodynamic points.

   barycentric coordinates
   barycentric
   homogeneous barycentric coordinates
   barycentrics
      Homogeneous coordinates (u : v : w) of a point P with respect to a reference triangle ABC, proportional to the signed areas of triangles PBC, PCA, and PAB.

   bilinear transformation
   bilinear transformations
   Möbius transformation
      A fractional linear or projective mapping of the form w = (az + b)/(cz + d) that preserves cross-ratios and maps circles and lines to circles and lines.

   Carnot's theorem
   Carnot theorem
      A theorem relating the signed distances from the circumcenter O to the sides of triangle ABC with the inradius r and circumradius R: d_a + d_b + d_c = R + r.

   centers of similitude
   center of similitude
   homothetic center
   similitude center
      The internal and external homothetic centers (:math:`S_+` and :math:`S_-`) of two circles, dividing the segment between their centers harmonically in the ratio of their radii.

   centroid
   medians
   median
      The point of concurrency G = X(2) of the three medians of a triangle, dividing each median in a 2:1 ratio with barycentric coordinates 1 : 1 : 1.

   Ceva's theorem
   cevian
   cevians
      A criterion for three cevians AD, BE, CF of triangle ABC to concur: (BD/DC)(CE/EA)(AF/FB) = 1.

   circumcenter
   circumcircle
   circumradius
      The center O = X(3) of the circumscribed circle of a triangle, located at the intersection of the perpendicular bisectors of the three sides.

   coaxial circles
   coaxial system
   pencil of circles
      A one-parameter pencil of circles in which every pair shares the same radical axis; classified into intersecting (elliptic), non-intersecting (hyperbolic with limiting points), and tangent (parabolic) pencils.

   Conway triangle notation
   Conway notation
   Conway symbols
      John H. Conway's algebraic system for triangle geometry setting S = 2K (twice the triangle area) and S_A = (b^2 + c^2 - a^2)/2 = bc cos A, S_B = (c^2 + a^2 - b^2)/2, S_C = (a^2 + b^2 - c^2)/2.

   cyclic quadrilateral
   cyclic quad
      A four-sided polygon inscribed in a single circle, satisfying opposite angle supplementary relations, Ptolemy's theorem, and Brahmagupta's area formula.

   ellipse
   elliptical
      A conic section with eccentricity 0 <= e < 1 whose points have a constant sum of distances 2a from two fixed foci.

   equilateral triangle
   equilateral
      A regular three-sided polygon with equal sides a = b = c and interior angles of 60 degrees, constructed in Euclid's Proposition I.1.

   Euler line
      The line passing through a non-equilateral triangle's orthocenter H, nine-point center N, centroid G, and circumcenter O, with HG : GN : NO = 4 : 1 : 2.

   excircle
   excircles
   excenter
   excenters
   exradius
   extangent
      One of three circles tangent to one side of a triangle and the extensions of the other two sides, centered at an excenter I_a, I_b, or I_c.

   Fermat point
   Torricelli point
   first Fermat point
      The isogonic center F = X(13) minimizing the sum of distances PA + PB + PC to the vertices of a triangle with all angles < 120 degrees.

   geometric mean
   mean proportional
      The mean proporional sqrt(ab) between two positive lengths a and b, constructed as the altitude to the hypotenuse of a right triangle with segments a and b.

   golden ratio
   divine proportion
   golden section
      The extreme and mean ratio phi = (1 + sqrt(5))/2 ≈ 1.6180339887, satisfying phi^2 = phi + 1.

   golden triangle
   golden triangles
   golden gnomon
      An isosceles triangle whose side-to-base ratio is the golden ratio phi, having vertex angles of 36-72-72 degrees (acute) or 108-36-36 degrees (gnomon).

   harmonic conjugate
   harmonic conjugates
   harmonic range
   harmonic division
      Given collinear points A, B, C, the point D is the harmonic conjugate of C with respect to (A, B) when the cross-ratio (A, B; C, D) = -1, so AC/CB = AD/BD.

   harmonic mean
      The reciprocal of the arithmetic mean of reciprocals, H = 2ab/(a + b), realized geometrically by the segment separating harmonic conjugates.

   Heron's formula
   Heron formula
      The expression for the area K of a triangle in terms of its semiperimeter s = (a+b+c)/2: K = sqrt(s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)), or in biquadratic side form 16K^2 = 2a^2b^2 + 2b^2c^2 + 2c^2a^2 - a^4 - b^4 - c^4.

   incenter
   incircle
   inradius
      The center I = X(1) of the inscribed circle of a triangle, located at the concurrency of the three interior angle bisectors with barycentric coordinates a : b : c.

   inversion
   inversive geometry
   circle inversion
      A transformation with respect to a circle of center O and radius R mapping each point P to P' on ray OP such that OP * OP' = R^2.

   isosceles triangle
   isosceles
      A triangle with at least two equal sides and two equal base angles (Euclid I.5, Pons Asinorum).

   isotomic conjugate
   isotomic
      The point P' obtained by reflecting the cevian traces of P across the midpoints of the corresponding sides, with barycentric coordinates 1/u : 1/v : 1/w.

   Menelaus's theorem
   Menelaus theorem
   transversal
      A collinearity criterion for three points D, E, F on the (extended) sides BC, CA, AB of triangle ABC: (BD/DC)(CE/EA)(AF/FB) = -1.

   nine-point circle
   Feuerbach circle
   nine-point center
      Feuerbach's circle centered at N = X(5) of radius R/2 passing through the three side midpoints, the three altitude feet, and the three Euler midpoints between the vertices and the orthocenter.

   orthic triangle
      The pedal triangle H_A H_B H_C of the orthocenter H, formed by the feet of the three altitudes of triangle ABC; it minimizes perimeter among all inscribed triangles of an acute triangle (Fagnano's problem).

   orthocenter
   altitude
   altitudes
      The concurrency H = X(4) of the three altitudes of a triangle, with barycentric coordinates tan A : tan B : tan C = 1/S_A : 1/S_B : 1/S_C.

   orthogonal circles
   orthogonality
      Two circles that intersect at right angles, so that the radii to their intersection points are tangent to the other circle and d^2 = r_1^2 + r_2^2.

   parabola
   parabolas
      A conic section of eccentricity e = 1 defined as the locus of points equidistant from a focus F and a directrix line.

   pedal triangle
   pedal triangles
      The triangle formed by the perpendicular projections of a point P onto the three sides of reference triangle ABC.

   pole and polar
   pole
   polar
   pole-polar
      A projective duality with respect to a conic or circle mapping a point P (the pole) to the line of harmonic conjugates or chord of contact (the polar).

   power of a point
   chord intersection
      For a point P and circle of center O and radius R, the invariant quantity Pi(P) = OP^2 - R^2 equal to the signed product PA * PB along any secant line through P.

   Pythagorean triple
   Pythagorean triples
      A tuple of positive integers (a, b, c) satisfying a^2 + b^2 = c^2, parametrized by Euclid's formula a = m^2 - n^2, b = 2mn, c = m^2 + n^2.

   radical axis
   radical axes
   radical center
      The locus of points having equal power with respect to two non-concentric circles, forming a line perpendicular to the line of centers.

   Stewart's theorem
   Stewart theorem
      A theorem relating the length d of a cevian AD dividing side BC = a into segments m and n: b^2 m + c^2 n = a(d^2 + mn).

   symmedian point
   symmedian
   Lemoine point
      The Lemoine point K = X(6), the concurrency of the reflections of the medians across the angle bisectors, with barycentric coordinates a^2 : b^2 : c^2.

   trilinear coordinates
   trilinear
   trilinears
      Homogeneous coordinates (alpha : beta : gamma) proportional to the signed directed distances from a point P to the sidelines BC, CA, AB, related to barycentrics by (u : v : w) = (a*alpha : b*beta : c*gamma).

   vesica piscis
   vesica
      The lens-shaped intersection of two congruent circles each passing through the other's center, fundamental to Euclid I.1 and Gothic pointed arch tracery with height-to-width ratio sqrt(3).
