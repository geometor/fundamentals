=======
Mission
=======

``geometor.fundamentals`` has a dual mission within the **GEOMETOR** ecosystem:

1. **Preserve the Fred W. Seelig Archive**: Curate, index, and transcribe the
   **353 handwritten geometry notebook pages** across **55 topics** and the
   **59 computational worksheets** (`Triangles and Circles.ods`,
   `circumcircle-circumcenter.ods`, `triangle-basics-draw.ods`) created by
   :person:`seelig` (:cite:`SeeligNotebooks`, :cite:`SeeligSpreadsheets`).
2. **Synthesize a Constructive Geometry Book Codex**: Transform every notebook
   study into an executable, symbolically verified 3-Track Book Codex
   (**Synthesis**, **Folios**, **Code**) using ``geometor.model`` and ``sympy``,
   creating a unified bridge between **Euclid's Elements** (``geometor/euclid``),
   **GEOMETOR Elements** (``geometor/elements``), and the **Encyclopedia of
   Triangle Centers** (``geometor/etc``).
