**Diplomatic Transcription — Folio 0118** (``0118.jpg``, Sheet 1 of 4):

Sets up bounding box :math:`(a, b)`, diagonals :math:`L_1, L_2`, normals :math:`L_3, L_4` meeting at :math:`C_1, C_2, C_3`, and end-cap circles of radius :math:`r`.
